Pastor James M. Kelly


James M. Kelly was born to the late Michael A. Kelly and Sherlie Kelly, in Washington, D.C. James is the oldest of three children. From his childhood James was reared and influenced by traditional Christian values instilled in him by a rich Christian heritage.

Pastor Kelly graduated from the Roosevelt Senior High School. He has attended Howard University and the University of North Carolina. Pastor Kelly is currently matriculating at the Maple Springs Baptist Bible College and Seminary.

Pastor Kelly knew he was called into the Gospel Ministry at an early age, however, he did not accept the call until later on in his life. He was licensed by the New Hope Baptist Church on June 2, 1990 and ordained by the same on May 24, 1995.

Pastor Kelly has served in several capacities during his ministry thus far. Some of those capacities are Assistant Pastor, Interim Pastor, Recording Secretary for the Baptist Association, and Pastor of the Jubilee Junction Christian Fellowship.

Pastor Kelly is married to Michelle D. Kelly; They have yet to be blessed with children to their union Yet.

He is the President and Founder of Jubilee Junction Ministries. JJCF is an evangelistic outreach ministry committed to preaching and teaching the uncompromised word of God to the ends of the earth. Ministry services include: revivals, seminars, leadership training, counseling, personal financial management, and mentoring young ministers in the gospel.

Pastor Kelly is a gifted preacher and teacher of the Gospel. He is known for his dynamic and engaging preaching style, which combines humor, personal anecdotes, and deep theological insights. He is passionate about helping people grow in their faith and apply biblical principles to their daily lives.
